Strapi's March to June 2026 releases (versions 5.40 through 5.48) shipped a built-in MCP server, a five-CVE security disclosure, a customizable Blocks editor, live media preview, and key relation fixes. The team merged hundreds of pull requests during the window, with roughly 22% contributed by the community.
